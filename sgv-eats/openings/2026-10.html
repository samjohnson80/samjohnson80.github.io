New in the SGV · October 2026

Forty-five names. A filing is not an opening.

This is the first roundup. It covers California ABC liquor-license filings from late August through October 2026 for San Gabriel Valley and nearby Inland Empire cities, plus a few places in that same stack that are not license filings. Duplicate license types for one address are one card. Applicant names, emails, and phone numbers are not on this page.

A public page matches the name and address

These are the places where a site, a directory, Eater, OpenTable, or a chamber notice names the restaurant at the street below. “Open” is used only in that case.
