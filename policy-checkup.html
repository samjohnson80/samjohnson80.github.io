What stays on this phone

Your file is read in this browser. It is not uploaded, not emailed, and not stored by me. I don’t run analytics on it. If you leave the page, it forgets the file and the answers. Choosing a file may download reading tools: pdf.js from cdnjs for a text PDF, and, for a photo or a scan, Tesseract from jsDelivr. Those downloads are the program, not your policy. The English reader may be kept in this browser so the next photo is faster. The policy is not. If you text me or send the summary to your bookkeeper, your own text or mail app sends that note. It lists the lines, not the file.

Before you rely on it

Does my policy leave this phone?

No. The file is read in this browser. It is not uploaded, emailed, or stored. If you text Sam or share the summary, that message is the list of lines you choose to send, not the file. Your phone’s text or mail app sends it.

If a coverage isn’t listed, does that mean I don’t have it?

No. The page can only say what it could read. A declarations page does not list every form, and workers’ comp is often a separate policy. “Not found on what you uploaded” means not found on those pages, not missing from the policy.
