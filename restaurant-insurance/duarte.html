Who signs off on a Duarte restaurant

Health permit and plan check
LA County Public Health, Environmental Health. A new build, a conversion, or a change of ownership with remodeling goes through plan check first. Initial review runs about 20 business days. Then building and safety, a final inspection, and the public health permit fee.1,2
Letter grade
LA County’s A, B, or C card, posted where the public can see it.3
Fire
LA County Fire. Duarte is one of the cities the county fire district serves.4,5
Alcohol
ABC’s Monrovia district office for northeastern LA County, now in Cerritos.6
Staff
A Certified Food Protection Manager, plus a food handler card for food employees within 30 days of hire.7,8

Workers’ comp for a small crew

California Labor Code section 3700 says every employer secures workers’ comp.9 The state’s plain-English FAQ is clear that part-time counts.10 A cousin who works two Saturday shifts for pay counts. Going without can mean a stop order and a $1,500 penalty per uninsured employee.11

When the policy gets written, the class code has to match the work:

  • 9080, full service. Someone greets, seats, takes the order at the table, serves, and drops the check.12
  • 9083, fast food or fast casual. Guests order at a counter, window, kiosk, or app and pay then.13

A counter place written as full service, or the reverse, is a problem you find at audit time. Text me a photo of your dec page and the workers’ comp page behind it. I’ll tell you if the class and the payroll look like your restaurant.
