Restaurant insurance by city

Your city decides who signs off. I can help with the insurance part.

Pasadena has its own health department. Rancho Cucamonga is a different county. Sierra Madre’s fire department inspects businesses every year. Pick your city and I’ll tell you who you’re dealing with and what to have on the policy.

  • Monrovia

    Monrovia is home base. If you run a place on Myrtle, on Huntington, or anywhere in between, lunch is close and so is a straight answer.

  • Arcadia

    Plenty of Arcadia restaurants lease space in shopping centers, from landlords with lawyers. The insurance section of that lease is the assignment. The policy is the homework.

  • Pasadena

    Pasadena is one of the few cities in LA County with its own public health department. That changes your opening timeline, and it changes what a takeover looks like.

  • Duarte

    Huntington Drive runs from my office straight into Duarte. If you run a small crew, let’s start with the policy small crews most often get wrong.

  • Sierra Madre

    A small downtown with its own fire department and a lot of hillside behind it. Both show up in how a Sierra Madre restaurant gets insured.

  • Azusa

    In a college town, half your staff might be new every fall. The state has deadlines for new hires who pour or handle food, and your insurance cares about the same people.

  • Glendora

    Glendora sits right up against the foothills. Insuring the stuff is the easy part. Insuring the weeks without sales is what keeps a place open.

  • Rancho Cucamonga

    Rancho Cucamonga is in San Bernardino County. If you are coming from an LA County restaurant, almost every office on your opening list changes.
