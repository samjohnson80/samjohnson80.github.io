Restaurant insurance in Glendora: what happens the week you’re closed.

Glendora sits right up against the foothills. Insuring the stuff is the easy part. Insuring the weeks without sales is what keeps a place open.

Glendora is a quick run east on the 210 from my office in Monrovia. When I sit down with a Glendora owner, I ask about the building first and the menu second.

Who signs off on a Glendora restaurant

Health permit and plan check
LA County Public Health, Environmental Health. A new build, a conversion, or a change of ownership with remodeling goes through plan check first. Initial review runs about 20 business days. Then building and safety, a final inspection, and the public health permit fee.1,2
Letter grade
LA County’s A, B, or C card, posted where customers can see it.3
Fire
LA County Fire, which serves Glendora and lists a regional office at 231 W. Mountain View Ave. in Glendora.4,5
Alcohol
ABC’s Monrovia district office for northeastern LA County, now in Cerritos.6
Workers’ comp
Required from your first employee, part-time included.7,8

Business income, in plain English

If a grease fire, a burst line, or a covered loss next door closes you, property coverage repairs the room and replaces the equipment. Business income coverage pays for the sales you lost while you were closed. They are different coverages with different limits.

  • The waiting period. Most policies do not start paying lost income on day one. Find the number of hours on your dec page.
  • The limit. Decide how many weeks of sales you could actually survive without. Then check whether the limit gets you there.
  • Payroll. Ask whether the coverage keeps paying your key people while you are closed. Good cooks do not wait around.
  • The address. Look yours up on CAL FIRE’s fire hazard severity zone map.9 It tells us how the property conversation will go before a carrier does.
