Who signs off on a Monrovia restaurant

Health permit and plan check
LA County Public Health, Environmental Health. A new build, a conversion, or a change of ownership with remodeling goes through plan check first. Initial review runs about 20 business days. Then building and safety, a final inspection, and the public health permit fee.1,2
Letter grade
LA County grades on inspection: A is 90 to 100, B is 80 to 89, C is 70 to 79. The card goes where the public can see it near the entrance.3
Fire
Monrovia has its own fire department. It is not one of the cities LA County Fire serves,4 so your hood suppression and extinguisher paperwork goes to the city.
Alcohol
ABC’s Monrovia district office, which now sits in Cerritos.5
City
A City of Monrovia business license.6 Ask Planning whether the space is zoned for a restaurant before you sign anything.

One small thing that confuses people: the ABC office that covers northeastern LA County is still called the Monrovia district office, but it is not in Monrovia anymore. ABC lists it at 12750 Center Court Dr., Suite 720, in Cerritos.5 If you drive to Monrovia looking for it, you will find me instead. Happy to help either way.

If you are already open, start here

Pull out the declarations page. Read three lines on it with me:

  • Business income. If a kitchen fire closes you for six weeks, property coverage fixes the room. Lost sales are their own coverage, with their own limit and their own waiting period. If that line is missing, that is the first thing I would talk about.
  • Liquor liability. If you pour beer or wine under a Type 41, or a full bar under a Type 47, general liability usually does not pick up the alcohol claim.7 Look for a separate liquor line or policy.
  • Workers’ comp. Required once you have one employee, part-time included.8,9 Going without can mean a stop order and $1,500 per uninsured employee.10
