Restaurant insurance in Pasadena, where the health department is the city’s own.

Pasadena is one of the few cities in LA County with its own public health department. That changes your opening timeline, and it changes what a takeover looks like.

Who signs off on a Pasadena restaurant

Health permit and plan check
The City of Pasadena Public Health Department, Environmental Health, not LA County. Printed plans are submitted in person, with the fee due at submittal. Review takes 20 business days; resubmittals take 10.1,2
Fire
The Pasadena Fire Department.3 Pasadena is not on LA County Fire’s list of cities it serves.4
Alcohol
ABC’s Monrovia district office for northeastern LA County, now located in Cerritos.5
Staff
A Certified Food Protection Manager and food handler cards are state law, not just county rules.6,7

Pasadena takeovers move fast. Your policy has to keep up.

Here is the Pasadena detail that matters most for insurance. The city’s plan check guide says you do not need plan review if you are taking over an existing permitted food business that stayed open, with no changes.2 That is good news. It also means a sale can close quickly, and the insurance is the piece people forget.

  • The seller’s policy is in the seller’s name. It does not become yours with the keys, the recipes, or the staff. Have your own policy start the day ownership changes.
  • Workers’ comp starts with your first payroll. If you keep the seller’s crew, you are their employer now. California requires coverage from the first employee, part-time included.8,9
  • Liquor licenses do not ride along by themselves. A license transfer goes through ABC, with its own public notice.10 Your liquor liability has to match the license you actually hold on opening night.
  • Use the old dec page as a map. Text it to me. It shows what the last owner carried and what they skipped. It is not your coverage.

If you are changing the space, the clock is different: printed plans go in person to the Pasadena Public Health Department, the fee is due at submittal, first review takes 20 business days, and resubmittals take 10. No construction until plans are approved.1,2
