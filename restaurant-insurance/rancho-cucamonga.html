I am happy to drive out from Monrovia for a Rancho Cucamonga owner, and lunch is still on me. The insurance is the same idea out here. The permits are not. Here is who you are actually dealing with.

Who signs off on a Rancho Cucamonga restaurant

Health permit and plan check
San Bernardino County Environmental Health Services, not LA County. The county lists a Rancho Cucamonga office at 8575 Haven Ave., Suite 130. Submit three paper plan sets or one PDF. Review takes up to 20 business days.1,2
Letter grade
San Bernardino County grades too: A is 90 to 100, B is 80 to 89, C is 70 to 79.3
Fire
The Rancho Cucamonga Fire Protection District. Hood and suppression plans go through the city’s permit center.4,5
Alcohol
ABC’s Riverside district office, 3737 Main St., Suite 900, covers San Bernardino County outside the desert areas.6

Why the order matters out here

San Bernardino County says building permits cannot be issued until Environmental Health approves your plans.2 Approved plans are good for two years, and the county inspects during and after construction before the health permit is issued.2 The county also offers a free initial plan check consultation.2 Use it.

Insurance shows up in that sequence earlier than people expect:

  • The landlord wants a certificate before you get the keys for construction. That usually means general liability, with the landlord as additional insured, before the first contractor shows up.
  • Builder’s risk or your property policy needs to cover the buildout while it is going in, not just after you open.
  • Workers’ comp starts with the first employee you pay, even if you have not opened yet.7,8
  • Liquor liability starts the night you pour, and has to match the license ABC issues: a Type 41 or a Type 47.9
