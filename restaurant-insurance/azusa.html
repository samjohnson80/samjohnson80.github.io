Restaurant and bar insurance in Azusa, for a crew that turns over.

In a college town, half your staff might be new every fall. The state has deadlines for new hires who pour or handle food, and your insurance cares about the same people.

Azusa has a university, a Metro line, and a lot of people who want a beer and dinner close to both. If you pour, this page is mostly for you. If you do not, skip to workers’ comp; it applies either way.

Who signs off on an Azusa restaurant

Health permit and plan check
LA County Public Health, Environmental Health. A new build, a conversion, or a change of ownership with remodeling goes through plan check first. Initial review runs about 20 business days. Then building and safety, a final inspection, and the public health permit fee.1,2
Letter grade
LA County’s A, B, or C card, posted where customers can see it.3
Fire
LA County Fire. Azusa is one of the cities that contracts with the county fire district for service.4
Alcohol
ABC’s Monrovia district office for northeastern LA County, now in Cerritos.5

Deadlines for new hires

  • RBS within 60 days. Anyone who serves alcohol on-premises, and the managers who oversee them, must finish Responsible Beverage Service training and pass ABC’s exam within 60 calendar days of starting. Certification lasts three years.6
  • Food handler card within 30 days. Food employees need a California Food Handler Card, and the employer pays for the training time and cost.7,8
  • Workers’ comp from day one. Part-time students count as employees.9,10

Liquor liability is not in your general liability

A Type 41 is beer and wine at a restaurant. A Type 47 is a full bar at a bona fide eating place.11 Either way, general liability usually does not pick up alcohol claims, which is why a separate liquor policy exists. California shields sellers from most suits over what an intoxicated adult does afterward,12 but not when the guest was an obviously intoxicated minor.13 ABC also notes that negligent service can still lead to a lawsuit.14 Being shielded does not stop someone from suing, so you still want a defense.
