Restaurant insurance in Sierra Madre, at the foot of the mountains.

A small downtown with its own fire department and a lot of hillside behind it. Both show up in how a Sierra Madre restaurant gets insured.

Who signs off on a Sierra Madre restaurant

Health permit and plan check
LA County Public Health, Environmental Health. A new build, a conversion, or a change of ownership with remodeling goes through plan check first. Initial review runs about 20 business days. Then building and safety, a final inspection, and the public health permit fee.1,2
Letter grade
LA County’s A, B, or C card, posted where customers can see it.3
Fire
The Sierra Madre Fire Department, which handles fire prevention, plan reviews, and annual business inspections.4
Alcohol
ABC’s Monrovia district office for northeastern LA County, now in Cerritos.5
Property
Check the address on CAL FIRE’s fire hazard severity zone map before you shop property coverage.6

The building is the hard part up here

I will not pretend every Sierra Madre address is a wildfire account. I will ask, because carriers do. Before we talk, look up your address on CAL FIRE’s fire hazard severity zone map.6 It takes a minute and it tells us which conversation we are having.

  • If you lease, the landlord usually insures the structure and you insure what is inside: your equipment, your stock, your buildout. Make sure that buildout number is a replacement number.
  • If you own the building and the only property option is the California FAIR Plan, treat it as the property policy it is. The Department of Insurance approved commercial limits of $20 million per building.7 It is not liability coverage. You still need general liability, liquor if you pour, and workers’ comp beside it.
  • Keep the fire paperwork. Sierra Madre’s fire department does plan reviews and annual business inspections.4 Underwriters ask for the same hood suppression service records the inspector does.
